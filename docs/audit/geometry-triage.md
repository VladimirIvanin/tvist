### Center, peek and empty-space contract boundary

`center:true` without loop may intentionally leave space at the finite first/last cards; `center.focus` trims finite edges. Those cases must not use a blanket full-viewport coverage oracle. Here SRC-G10 has `center:false`, and SRC-G03/SRC-G11 are loop layouts with more total content than the viewport. [Loop documentation](../examples/loop.md#loop--perpage-3-peek-120-4-слайда) explicitly says even the small-count peek example must not expose a hole; [center+loop](../examples/center.md#center--loop-бесконечное-центрирование) promises an infinite centered carousel. The large settled holes are therefore not excused as intentional finite-edge centering. Peek padding itself and genuinely clipped CSS gaps are excluded appropriately.

No physical Safari/iPhone/Android, compositor pixel correctness, native touch scrolling, or source fixes are claimed. Geometry comparisons in the final source cases use tolerance at most 1 CSS px. A failed early oracle never certifies the rest of its planned action sequence.
